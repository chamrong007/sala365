# SALA365

SALA365 is a technology academy and consulting center focused on practical
learning, professional pathways, and workforce development across cloud,
networking, Linux, cybersecurity, AI, and modern workplace technologies.

The website is a single-page introduction to SALA365's vision, mission,
learning pathways, featured courses, and support for learners and organizations
in Cambodia.

## Training registration

Course questions and registration guidance are available through
[`@Sala365Bot`](https://t.me/Sala365Bot). The student registration form is
available at [Microsoft Forms](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/iywsNMEwHw).

See [`docs/training-registration-workflow.md`](docs/training-registration-workflow.md)
for the Microsoft Forms fields, Power Automate email workflow, confirmation
email template, administration notification template, and deployment test
checklist. The flow and Telegram bot behavior must be configured in their
respective Microsoft and Telegram accounts.
